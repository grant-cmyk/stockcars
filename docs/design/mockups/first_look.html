Weapon trucks: build one, then race it round the oval

16 Nov 2026, 21:24 SAST. Pictures only. Nothing here is game code, and none of it is saved in a repository yet.

Changed in version 3: the track is now bigger than the screen and the view follows your truck (section 1). Changed from version 1: the arena is now an oval track with short straights. You race laps, and every truck carries a weapon and a defence. These are true Spectrum screens: 256 by 192 pixels, two colours in each 8 by 8 square.

1. The scrolling view

The same drive, twice. The oval is about three screens wide. The small outline at the bottom right is the map of the whole track, with your truck flashing.

  • I recommend A, since you are happy with the Breakfast Run engine and it scrolls this way.
  • The cost of scrolling: colour on the Spectrum can only move 8 pixels at a time. So the grass is drawn as green dots on black, and no square may hold both grass and a white line. The black run-off is what keeps them apart.
  • Rivals more than a screen away are only visible on the map.

2. The whole oval, for reference

You would never see the track like this in the game. It shows the shape, a weapon hit, and the obstacles.

  • The Breakfast Run racetrack look: black road, dashed white limit line, black run-off, green grass.
  • Watch the pink truck on the top straight. The yellow hammer truck catches it, it spins, and its armour bar drops.
  • Obstacles, not too many: an oil patch on the left bend and barrels beside the top straight.
  • Side panel: race order, each truck's weapon, and its armour.
  • Colour clash you can see here: when two trucks touch, they share squares and one takes the other's colour for a moment. A truck also tints the white dashes it drives over. Both are the real machine's behaviour.

3. Build your truck

  • Pick a body, wheels, weapon and defence. Each part changes speed, turning, hit power and toughness.
  • A weight limit stops you taking the best of everything, so a heavy hammer costs you speed on the straights.

4. The weapons

Spike, hammer, tipper and saw, each shown at rest and in use. These are my rough stand-ins. You can redraw them in the pixel editor, as you did with the Breakfast Run cars.

What I need you to decide

  1. Scrolling A or B? I recommend A, smooth.
  2. What happens to a truck with no armour left? Out of the race, or stopped for a few seconds and then back in with a little armour.
  3. How do you win? I have assumed first to finish the laps.
  4. One player or two? Two on one keyboard would suit this game well.
  5. The name.

Not checked: how it feels to drive, and sound. The lettering is a stand-in for the Spectrum's own letters, and the trucks look ragged when turned because they have not been hand-drawn at each angle.
