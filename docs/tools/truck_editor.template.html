Truck editor

One truck body is shared by every truck. Each weapon is drawn on top of it. Pick a part, draw, then press Copy the art and paste it back to Claude.

1. Pick the part to draw

2. Pick a pen and draw

Colour is drawn in the truck's colour. Solid black is part of the truck and hides the road behind it. Rubbed-out squares are see-through. The other layer shows faintly so you can line things up.

Top: four times size. Middle: double size on the road. Bottom: real size, in the four truck colours.
